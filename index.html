1. Introduction and who we are

chrip (Android package: com.chrip.app) is a calm, minimal chat app for learning and work. Teachers run batches (classes), and students join with an invite code. The app includes 1-to-1 and group chat, fee tracking, attendance, homework, photo sharing, and push notifications.

chrip is operated by Chrip, based in India. There are four kinds of users: Teacher, Student, Member, and Guest (anonymous). This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and the choices you have. By using chrip, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Account data

Your name, email address, role, an optional profile photo, and sign-in details handled by Firebase Authentication. Guests use an anonymous account with no name or email.

Content you create

Chat messages, photos you send, homework notes and photos, and batch details.

Fee records

Fee plans, due status, and the UPI transaction reference a student types in.

chrip does not process payments. We do not collect bank account or card details. Fees are paid outside the app, directly between students and teachers.

Attendance and homework

Attendance records and homework submissions inside batches.

Device and technical data

Push notification tokens, and basic diagnostic and crash information that helps us find and fix problems.

Activity data

Your online status and last seen time. You can turn this off in Settings.

3. How we use information

We use your information to:

  • run the app and keep your account working;
  • deliver messages and push notifications;
  • let teachers manage batches, fees, attendance, and homework;
  • keep the service secure and prevent misuse;
  • fix bugs and improve reliability; and
  • meet our legal duties.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not show ads.

4. Who can see your information

Other users see your information only as the app is designed to allow:

  • Members of a chat can see the messages in that chat.
  • A teacher can see the fees, attendance, and homework of the members in their batches.
  • A student can see only their own fee, attendance, and homework records.
  • The full attendance register is visible only to the teacher.

We do not share your personal data with anyone else, except the service providers described below and where the law requires it.

5. Third-party services

chrip is built on Google Firebase. We use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Cloud Functions. Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers and handled by Google as our service provider.

6. Data storage, security, and retention

Your data is encrypted in transit, and access is limited by security rules so that people can reach only what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

We keep your data while your account is active. After you delete your account, we delete your data, except where the law requires us to keep it. Backups are removed within a reasonable period.

7. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account inside the app: open Settings, then tap Delete account.

What is removed: your profile, your photo, your device tokens, and your membership in batches and chats.

What remains: messages you sent to other people stay in their chats, but your name is replaced with “Deleted user” as the sender.

If you cannot use the in-app option, email us at [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] to request deletion. We handle these requests within 30 days.

A teacher with active batches must archive or delete them before deleting their account.

8. Children and students under 18

chrip is used for tutoring, so some students may be minors. The service is not directed at children under 13. Minors should use chrip with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian. Teachers are responsible for adding students only with that consent.

Parents or guardians can contact us at [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] to review or delete a child’s data.

9. Your rights

In line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data;
  • withdraw your consent; and
  • have a grievance addressed.

To use these rights, change your details or delete your account in the app’s Settings, or email [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL].

10. Notifications and permissions

  • Notifications: optional. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.
  • Photos: chrip uses the system photo picker, so it can access only the photos you choose.
  • Contacts, location, microphone, and camera: chrip does not access these. If we add any of them later, we will update this policy first.

11. International transfers

Your data may be processed on Google servers outside India. Google applies its own security and privacy safeguards to the data it handles for us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date at the bottom of this page, and we will show an in-app notice for major changes.
